The forecasting problem

The competition required hourly wind power forecasts for sites in Gyeongju, Yangyang, and Yeongdeok. Forecast quality was evaluated using both prediction error and an economic settlement metric.

A hybrid physics–ML approach

Our team used physics-based feature engineering, including air-density estimation informed by the Arden–Buck equation, and a physical power curve as a baseline prediction. A residual LightGBM model corrected remaining errors. The final ensemble combined Ridge Regression, Random Forest, and residual LightGBM, with weights optimized using Optuna.

My contribution

I worked on model architecture design and hyperparameter optimization. Derrick Junsu Lee led the team and worked on machine learning and testing; Hyunseung Lee worked on preprocessing and feature engineering.

Outcome

Zephyr Analytics received third place (Excellence Award) among 270 teams. The reported final metrics were 19.34% NMAE and a 22.09% settlement revenue rate.
