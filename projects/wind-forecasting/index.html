Energy Forecasting

Physics-Guided Wind Power Prediction

BDA × EWP × 60Hertz · Zephyr Analytics · 2025 · 3rd Place · Excellence Award

Hourly wind-power forecasting with a physical power curve, LightGBM residual correction, and an optimized ensemble.

Official competition
Overview / Methodology / Results / My contribution

Overview

Forecast hourly generation for Gyeongju, Yangyang, and Yeongdeok. Evaluate both prediction error and settlement revenue.

Methodology

01 Physical baseline

Start with a turbine power curve. Estimate expected generation from the weather conditions.

  • Atmospheric features: Include air-density estimates. The team used the Arden–Buck equation in feature construction.
  • Baseline role: Use the physical relationship as the starting forecast.

Sources: Team architecture slide

02 Residual learning

Compute the error between observed generation and the physical baseline. Train LightGBM to predict that residual.

  • Correction: Add the predicted residual to the baseline.
  • Model structure: The learned component corrects the physical forecast’s remaining error.

hybrid forecast = physical baseline + learned residual

Sources: Team architecture slide

03 Ensemble optimization

Combine the hybrid forecast with Ridge Regression and Random Forest predictions.

  • Search: Use Optuna to tune model hyperparameters and ensemble weights.
  • Evaluation: Report NMAE and the competition’s settlement revenue rate. Inspect site-wise distributions alongside the aggregate scores.

Sources: Team evaluation slide
