Medical Imaging

Accelerated MRI Reconstruction

SNU FastMRI Challenge · Team 2rm1000 · 2025 · 3rd Place

Reconstruct brain and knee MRI images from undersampled multi-coil frequency measurements.

CodeChallenge2025 final leaderboardOfficial coverage2025 announcement

Problem

Acquiring fewer k-space samples accelerates MRI scans but leaves incomplete measurements. The task is to reconstruct an image while respecting the samples that were acquired.

  • Input: Multi-coil k-space data with 4× or 8× acceleration.
  • Output: Reconstructed brain or knee images.

My contribution

I worked on Fourier-domain modeling with Jinseok Choi in Team 2rm1000. The model pipeline and competition submission were team work.

Reconstruction method

1. Reconstruction with data consistency

Use Feature VarNet and FI VarNet to alternate learned reconstruction updates with the MRI measurement model.

  • Coil sensitivities: Estimate sensitivity maps from the multi-coil measurements.
  • Learned updates: Refine feature representations with Feature VarNet and include image-domain cascades in FI VarNet.
  • Data consistency: Connect each update to the acquired k-space samples through the MRI encoding model.

2. Anatomy- and acceleration-specific models

Train separate experts for each acquisition condition and select them with explicit inference rules.

  • Anatomy: Identify brain or knee inputs from coil count under the challenge data conventions.
  • Acceleration: Read the sampling mask to choose the 4× or 8× expert.

3. MRI-aware training

Train with acquisition-aware augmentation, AdamW, and warmup followed by cosine scheduling.

  • Augmentation: Apply small rotations, shearing, and horizontal flips. Increase their strength during training.
  • Memory: Use DeepSpeed and activation checkpointing for the reconstruction networks.
  • Checkpoints: Resume interrupted runs and convert distributed checkpoints into inference weights.

4. Weight averaging and output ensembling

Combine compatible checkpoints and complementary reconstruction models.

  • Weight averaging: Average model parameters into one checkpoint without adding inference passes.
  • Output ensembling: Average reconstructed images from configured Feature VarNet and FI VarNet pairs.
