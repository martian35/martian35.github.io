The challenge

A fully autonomous robot had to locate target classes announced at match time, collect the appropriate objects, and deliver them to a storage area. The system had to complete perception, navigation, collection, and delivery without operator input after the start signal.

Our system

The team built a mecanum-wheel platform with a Jetson Orin Nano, dual cameras, LiDAR, an IMU, and a passive collector. YOLO instance segmentation supplied visual detections, while navigation and geometric checks supported target selection and collection.

My contribution

I worked on perception and model training alongside Jinyong Jun. This was one part of the team system, alongside dedicated hardware, drivetrain, navigation, and strategy work.

Outcome

Team 7 placed second, earning 140 out of 200 points across the two final runs. The competition also provided the opportunity to attend the 2026 World Robot Conference.
