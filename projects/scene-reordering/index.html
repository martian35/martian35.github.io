Structured Scene Reordering with Multimodal LLMs

Recovering the chronological order of four shuffled frames from a text description using multimodal reasoning and structured permutation decoding.

The task

Given a natural-language storyline and four shuffled images, the model must recover a coherent chronological sequence. This requires reasoning across both visual observations and language, rather than recognizing each image independently.

Our approach

The documented submission uses Qwen3.5-27B with LoRA adaptation, structured pairwise and chronological readouts, and a decoder over the 24 possible permutations. Temporal-gap supervision and local hard-negative ranking support training, while Random4 aggregation and late-trajectory weight averaging form the final inference pipeline.

Competition result

GeekSeek finished eighth overall in SNU AI Challenge 2026. The linked repository distinguishes the official submission from experiments performed after the competition.
