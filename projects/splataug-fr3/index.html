My role

Responsible for the 3DGS data augmentation module within a larger collaborative dataset project.

The larger effort

This work contributes to an augmented robotic dataset that is being developed toward a publication. My responsibility is the 3D Gaussian Splatting data augmentation module, one component of the larger effort.

My contribution

The module turns captured workcell imagery and robot demonstrations into reusable 3D scene assets and rendered training views. The current implementation supports FR3 manipulation scenes and multiple calibrated camera viewpoints.

Current status

The broader dataset collaboration and its manuscript are in progress. Paper details and additional results will be added as the collaboration progresses.
