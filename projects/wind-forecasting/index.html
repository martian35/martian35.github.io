Energy Forecasting

Physics-Guided Wind Power Forecasting

BDA × EWP × 60Hertz · Zephyr Analytics · 2025 · 3rd Place · Excellence Award

Forecast hourly wind generation by correcting a physical power curve with learned residuals.

Official competition

Problem and approach

A turbine power curve gives a physical estimate of wind generation. The model learns the error that remains between this estimate and observed production.

  • Task: Predict hourly generation for Gyeongju, Yangyang, and Yeongdeok.
  • Evaluation: Measure forecast error and settlement revenue under the competition rules.

My contribution

I designed the model architecture and optimized hyperparameters in Team Zephyr Analytics.

Forecasting model

1. Physical baseline

Estimate generation from weather conditions and the turbine power curve.

  • Atmospheric features: Include air-density estimates, using the Arden–Buck equation in feature construction.
  • Physical structure: Use the power curve as the initial forecast before learning a correction.

2. Residual learning

Train LightGBM to predict the difference between observed generation and the physical baseline.

  • Training target: Subtract the baseline forecast from the observed generation.
  • Hybrid prediction: Add the learned correction back to the physical estimate.

hybrid forecast = physical baseline + learned residual

3. Ensemble and hyperparameter optimization

Combine the hybrid model with Ridge Regression and Random Forest forecasts.

  • Optimization: Use Optuna to tune model hyperparameters and ensemble weights.
  • Evaluation: Inspect site-wise generation distributions alongside NMAE and settlement revenue.
