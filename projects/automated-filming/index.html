Automated Filming Device

HD Hyundai Mechatronics Competition · 2024 · Grand Prize

A Raspberry Pi camera mount with automatic face tracking and infrared remote control.

Team codeDevice demoFilming result

Background and product idea

A solo creator needs to move without continually adjusting the camera. Our prototype keeps the subject in frame with two-axis rotation and provides manual control through an infrared remote.

My contribution

Team member on the filming-device prototype. The hardware, software, and demos below describe the shared team project.

Hardware and software

1. Hardware

A Raspberry Pi connects the camera, two servos, and infrared receiver.

  • Pan–tilt mount: Rotate the camera horizontally and vertically with separate servo axes.
  • Mechanical support: Use a ball-transfer unit to distribute weight and reduce friction.

2. Pixel error to incremental servo motion

Capture frames with Picamera2, run the Haar-cascade face detector, and select the largest detected face.

  • Feedback signal: Measure the selected face center relative to the image center and normalize by image width and height.
  • Control law: Multiply each normalized error by a proportional gain of 30 to obtain the incremental servo-angle command.
  • Actuation: Add the increment to the current angle, clamp it to the servo limits, and map it to calibrated pulse width.
  • Lost detection: Skip automatic updates when no valid face center is available and turn off the detection LED.

e_x = (W/2 − c_x) / W; e_y = (H/2 − c_y) / H Δθ_pan = 30 e_x; Δθ_tilt = 30 e_y

W and H are image dimensions; c_x and c_y are the face center. The released controller sets both derivative gains to zero.

3. Concurrent remote control and shutdown

Run the LIRC infrared reader in a separate thread alongside the camera loop.

  • Mode switch: KEY_0 switches automatic tracking on or off.
  • Manual motion: Directional keys start continuous motion on either axis; KEY_5 stops the servos.
  • Exit: KEY_9 ends the loop. Reset the camera pose, stop capture, disable servo pulses, and join the remote thread.
