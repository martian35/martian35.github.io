\section{Projects}
\project{Autonomous Object-Collecting Robot}{2026}{%
\href{https://martian35.github.io/projects/robot-challenge/}{Project}\enspace$|$\enspace
\href{https://github.com/junjinyong/snu-ai-robot-challenge-2026-team7}{GitHub}\par
Contributed to perception, planning, data collection, hardware, and system integration.
Our team combined dual-camera segmentation, geometric target selection, and LiDAR/IMU-based motion control.
The robot placed \textbf{2nd} in the SNU AI Robot Challenge, scoring \textbf{140/200} in the final.}

\project{Multimodal Scene Reordering}{2026}{%
\href{https://martian35.github.io/projects/scene-reordering/}{Project}\enspace$|$\enspace
\href{https://github.com/martian35/geekseek-snuai-2026}{GitHub}\par
Team GeekSeek adapted Qwen3.5-27B with LoRA to order four shuffled frames.
The submission scores valid permutations and aggregates predictions across input orderings.
Our team finished \textbf{8th overall} in the SNU AI Challenge.}

\project{Accelerated MRI Reconstruction}{2025}{%
\href{https://martian35.github.io/projects/fastmri/}{Project}\enspace$|$\enspace
\href{https://github.com/gates02/fastmri-2-1000}{GitHub}\par
Worked with Team 2rm1000 on reconstruction from undersampled k-space.
Our pipeline combines Feature/FI VarNet, data consistency, anatomy- and acceleration-specific models, and ensembling.
The team placed \textbf{3rd} in the SNU FastMRI Challenge at 4$\times$/8$\times$ acceleration.}

\project{Physics-Guided Wind Power Forecasting}{2025}{%
\href{https://martian35.github.io/projects/wind-forecasting/}{Project}\par
Designed model architecture and optimized hyperparameters in Team Zephyr Analytics.
We combined a physical turbine power curve with LightGBM residual learning and a weighted ensemble.
Our team placed \textbf{3rd out of 270 teams}, reporting \textbf{19.34\% NMAE}.}

\project{Automated Filming Device}{2024}{%
\href{https://martian35.github.io/projects/automated-filming/}{Project}\enspace$|$\enspace
\href{https://github.com/alvin0808/face-traker}{GitHub}\par
Built a hands-free filming prototype with a team.
The Raspberry Pi system combines face detection, proportional pan--tilt servo control, and infrared remote input.
The team received the \textbf{Grand Prize} in the HD Hyundai Mechatronics Competition.}
